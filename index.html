How It Works

Checklists on the floor, one view for management

Amusement Standards runs ride operations this way: inspectors complete checklists on their phones, and every submission lands in one management workspace. TableStandards uses the same model for restaurants.

  • Built for phones. Each checklist and tool is made for a phone in one hand, during service.
  • Everything rolls up. Every entry lands in the same restaurant record.
  • One view for the GM. Management sees every checklist from every station in one place.
  • And one for ownership. Owner’s Audit reads across that record for the people who own the room.

Guest Ratings

Your own rating system, at the table

A fast, in-house star rating that guests reach with a QR code. Ratings stay with you. Five-star guests are invited to post on Google.

  1. Scan. Each restaurant has its own QR code. Guests scan it and land on a clean rating screen.
  2. Rate. One tap to choose one to five stars.
  3. Five stars: the guest gets a one-tap link to your Google review page, plus an on-screen QR code.
  4. Under five: the guest fills out a private feedback form that goes only to you, not to Google.

Every rating lands in the restaurant record and rolls up to the management view.
