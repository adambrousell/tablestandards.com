The Composite Score

A FICO-type score for the dining room

Amusement Standards scores ride operations with one composite risk number. TableStandards applies the same idea to restaurants: each tool contributes a signal, and the signals combine into one score per restaurant that you can track over time.

  • Four tools, one ledger. Shift Report, PMIX, Cellar but no Somm, and Wine Portal each send their data into the same restaurant record.
  • Guests count too. In-house guest ratings are one of the score's inputs.
  • One score per restaurant. Each location gets one number, so you can compare locations and follow the trend.

Guest Ratings

Your own rating system, at the table

A fast, in-house star rating that guests reach with a QR code. Ratings stay with you. Five-star guests are invited to post on Google.

  1. Scan. Each restaurant has its own QR code. Guests scan it and land on a clean rating screen.
  2. Rate. One tap to choose one to five stars.
  3. Five stars: the guest gets a one-tap link to your Google review page, plus an on-screen QR code.
  4. Under five: the guest fills out a private feedback form that goes only to you, not to Google.

Every rating is stored with the restaurant's record and feeds its TableStandards score.
