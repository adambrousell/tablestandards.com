The Process

Film
Made
Together

No studio. No gatekeepers. Reel Oz runs entirely on footage from people who actually live, work, and breathe this city.

01
Shoot Anything. Shoot Everything.
Phone, mirrorless, GoPro — doesn't matter. If it's Asbury Park, we want it. Early morning Deal Lake fog. Saturday night on Cookman. A storm rolling in off the Atlantic. All of it.
Any device. Any format.
02
Drop It in the Portal
Upload your raw clips through our submission portal. Tag the location, time of day, and mood. One clip or a hundred — we'll take them all. No editing required on your end.
Upload · Tag · Done
03
Our Editors Go to Work
The Reel Oz editorial team digs through submissions and builds the film. We sequence, color grade, score, and cut — pulling the best from hundreds of contributors into one coherent piece.
Professional edit · Color · Score
04
Your Name in the Credits
Every contributor whose footage makes the final cut is credited. You're not a stock library — you're a co-director. Films screen locally, stream online, and run in AP venues.
Full contributor credit · Revenue share

Open Submissions

Your Footage Belongs Here.

Every frame of Asbury Park matters. The quiet Tuesday afternoon on Bangs Ave. The crowd spilling out of a show at 1 AM. The way the light hits Convention Hall at the exact wrong angle and makes it look perfect. We want all of it.

Upload Your Clips → Free to submit · Contributors credited · Revenue share available
What We're Looking For
  • 📍
    Location
    Must be Asbury Park, NJ or immediate surrounding area — Neptune, Bradley Beach, Ocean Grove.
  • 📱
    Any Device
    Phone footage, DSLR, mirrorless, GoPro, dashcam, Super 8 — all accepted. We grade in post.
  • 🎬
    Raw Preferred
    Send us the unedited original. Don't apply filters or crop first — our editors do that work.
  • ⏱
    Length
    5 seconds to 60 minutes. Longer raw footage = more material for our editors to work with.
  • ©
    Your Original Work
    Must be footage you shot. You retain ownership; we license usage with full credit given.

About Reel Oz

Not a
Studio.
A City.

Reel Oz isn't a production house with a lot. We're a collective — editors, musicians, and locals who believe the best documentary of Asbury Park is the one being shot right now by 300 people who call it home.

"The most honest film about a place is never made by an outsider with a budget. It's made by the people who were already there — before the cameras showed up."
